Messaging

Messaging owns outbound communication such as email while remaining independent from Users and Notifications.

Install

composer require tihloh/prefab-messaging

Concept

Your business action
      ↓
Prefab Messaging
      ↓
Configured transport/provider
      ↓
Email or other outbound channel

Credentials, sender identity and transport configuration belong in configuration, not directly in controllers.

Better together

Users may provide recipient details, Notifications may represent an in-app notice, and Messaging may deliver an email. They remain separate capabilities so you can install only what you need.

Checkpoint: configure a test transport and send one message before connecting it to a larger workflow.
